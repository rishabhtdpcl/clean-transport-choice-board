The Karnataka Clean Transport Network

In CA3.1 we found that no single solution can remove fuel dependence on its own. Our solution combines six parts that support each other.

Electric Buses

5,250

e-buses allocated to Karnataka (4,500 under PM E-DRIVE and 750 under PM-eBus Sewa).

EV Charging

5,765

public charging stations, the highest in India, with 4,462 in Bengaluru Urban.

Battery Swapping

₹10,000

subsidy per 200 swaps under the Karnataka Clean Mobility Policy 2025–30.

Renewable Charging

Solar

Solar panels at bus depots and parking areas can power EV chargers with clean electricity.

Ethanol Blending

E20

India reached 20% ethanol blending in petrol in 2025–26, reducing crude oil use.

Public Transport

1 bus

can replace dozens of private vehicles, which is the cheapest way to save fuel.

Why It Matters

Our project started with the global problem of crude oil supply volatility. In Karnataka, this problem reaches people mainly through transport fuel prices.

88.7%

of India's crude oil was imported in 2025–26.

34%

of the world's energy came from oil in 2024.

₹110.82

price of one litre of petrol in Bengaluru (6 Nov 2026).

Crude oil price rises → Petrol and diesel cost more → Transport costs rise → Prices of goods go up
